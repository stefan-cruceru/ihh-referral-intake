# In our language · En nuestro idioma

The staff campaign for **Lamplight**, the proposed name staff will see for the app in `app/`. The
code still calls it Care Bridge until the team agrees on the rename.

**Status: draft.** Before anything in this folder is printed, posted or read aloud:

- IHH's HR, compliance and counsel review the wording.
- A bilingual IHH staff member reads every Spanish line. The Spanish was drafted with AI.
- Someone runs a quick trademark search on the name "Lamplight".

Nothing in this folder contains patient information.

## The idea

IHH told us frontline staff worry that AI will replace them, some have ethical concerns, and many
don't see how it fits their work. So the campaign starts by listening. It speaks to each job in its
own words, in English and Spanish, and makes one promise everywhere:

> It never says yes or no. You do. · Nunca dice sí o no. Usted decide.

The name and the logo carry the same idea: a lamp left on at an empty desk. It stays up, so staff
don't have to. Lamplight keeps the list, the reminders and the simple paperwork. People keep every
decision, and go home.

## Who it speaks to

- **Hospital side, "Ending the frustration era, together":** referral coordinators, the clinical
  line, whoever covers intake at night, and, outside IHH, hospital case managers and their
  supervisors.
- **Onsite, "More time with clients":** LVNs and medical assistants, case managers, site
  operations, and the clients themselves.
- **Everyone at IHH**, including people who will never open the app.
