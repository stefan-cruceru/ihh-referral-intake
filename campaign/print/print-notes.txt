IN OUR LANGUAGE / EN NUESTRO IDIOMA - print kit for Lamplight

BEFORE PRINTING ANYTHING
- IHH's HR, compliance and counsel sign off on the wording.
- A bilingual IHH staff member reads every Spanish line. The Spanish was drafted with AI.
- Write names in the blanks by hand (the peer to ask, the leaders who sign the letter,
  the referral line and fax number). Nothing on these sheets holds patient information.

WHAT EACH FILE IS
01  Break-room poster ........... 18 x 24 in, one per break room. Print shop.
02  Referral office poster ....... 11 x 17 in (tabloid). Print shop or a tabloid printer.
03  "Who this is for" poster ..... 11 x 17 in, for the referral office or clinical line.
04  Desk cards ................... 6 x 4 in, 4 cards (fax desk, clinical line, nursing station,
                                   case management). Heavy cardstock.
05  Letter to our team ........... Letter size. English on page 1, Spanish on page 2.
                                   Print double-sided. Leaders sign by hand.
06-09  A letter to each job ...... Letter size, English / Spanish, double-sided:
                                   referral coordinators, clinical line, LVNs and MAs, case managers.
12  Letter for after-hours intake  Letter size, English / Spanish. For the director, lead and
                                   salaried staff who cover intake at night.
13  Letter for hospital supervisors  Letter size, English / Spanish. Goes to hospital case
                                   management supervisors (the reminder chain's last step).
                                   Write the referral line in the P.S. blank.
14  Letter for site operations ... Letter size, English / Spanish. For whoever gets the email
                                   that a new client is on the way. Write the desk's number in.
15  Letter for everyone at IHH ... Letter size, English / Spanish. For staff who won't use the
                                   app but will hear about it. Write a contact name in the P.S.
Every letter has two signature lines. Leaders sign by hand.
10  Hospital in-service sheet .... 5.5 x 8.5 in (half letter), English / Spanish.
                                   Handed out at in-services with the referral form and checklist.
11  Huddle guide ................. Letter size, English / Spanish. For whoever leads the
                                   5-minute huddle. Has lines to write down what people said.

PRINTING TIPS
- The posters use full-color backgrounds and are sized for a print shop. They have no bleed,
  so ask the shop to print to size, or let a thin white border show.
- The handouts are mostly white and read fine in black and white on an office printer.
- The client "Your next steps" sheet is not in this folder. Lamplight prints it for each client.
