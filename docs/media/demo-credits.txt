IHH Care Bridge — presentation demo

Two-minute working-app demonstration, based on the repository at:
https://github.com/stefan-cruceru/ihh-referral-intake
Source revision: e8f2a7b5ace01e73e393c7e275cdbbd1273a8d1c

The app recording uses the supplied fictional practice patients. Practice reviews
are seeded from the organizers' answer key and passed through the app's checks.
The recording does not demonstrate live AI extraction or measured time savings.
Human staff make decisions. All messages and handoffs shown are drafts.
No real IHH systems are connected. Voiceover is synthesized narration.

The opening and closing use illustrative stock footage of people at work.
These people are not represented as IHH staff or patients, or as endorsing the app.
