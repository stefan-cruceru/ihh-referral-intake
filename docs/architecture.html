What leaves the computer

  • Page images, to Claude. Flattened pictures of the packet, so active PDF parts never reach it. Answers come back as checked JSON.
  • Nothing else on its own. Emails, Teams cards and Kipu writes are drafts or buttons a signed-in person presses.
  • Later, with IHH's OK. Live Kipu, Teams sign-in and Power Automate are built and switched off.

The native web server listens on 127.0.0.1. Docker binds inside the container and publishes to the host's loopback interface. Packet pages stay in memory. The local audit view checks seals of entries still present; it does not prove that the newest entries or whole log were not removed.

Ships as ihh-care-bridge.exe (no admin rights) or docker compose up on port 8780.
