Sam's In our language / En nuestro idioma identity governs the film: Public Sans,
warm cream, brown, amber and terracotta, and her desk-lamp concept refined for
small sizes. The canonical logo lives in campaign/logo/ in the repository.

The recording uses only the supplied fictional practice patients. Practice reviews
are seeded from the organizers' answer key and passed through the app's checks.
This is not a recording of live AI extraction or measured time savings.
Lamplight helps staff work toward IHH's two-hour response goal; the goal is
aspirational and does not promise or guarantee a turnaround time.
Staff make decisions; only the clinical reviewer can deny. Messages and handoffs
shown are drafts. There are no real IHH system connections. Bilingual IHH review
and accuracy validation remain prerequisites for a pilot.

Voice: synthesized narration, Runway speech / Leslie, with a quiet original
synthesized underscore. English narration. English and Spanish narration
subtitles are optional sidecar files/player tracks; no narration captions are
burned into either video. The app and artwork retain their bilingual labels.

Opening and closing: illustrative licensed stock footage of people at work.
The people are not represented as IHH staff, patients or endorsers.
